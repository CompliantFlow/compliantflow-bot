PeakForm Sports: Demo Retailer

PeakForm Sports is a fictional sports equipment and apparel retailer created so you can test CompliantFlow in a realistic e-commerce scenario. The demo covers shipping, returns, sizing, product details, and support boundaries. It is designed to avoid personal data collection and to clearly disclose that you are interacting with an AI assistant, as required in the EU AI Act and the GDPR.
